# A Burst Pipe in a Brighton Flat: Who Do You Call First?

A burst pipe in a flat is not a single emergency — it's three, happening at once. Water is damaging your home, it's leaking into the flat below, and every minute you spend deciding who to call is another minute of water. The order you call people in matters.

## First: stop the water

Before any phone call, find your stopcock and turn it off. In most Brighton flats it's under the kitchen sink, in a hallway cupboard, or near the water meter. If you don't know where yours is, find it this weekend, before you need it — in an emergency, five minutes of searching is five minutes of water pouring through a ceiling.

## Second: call your plumber

A burst pipe needs a plumber, not a handyman and not a wait until morning. Describe what you see: where the water is coming from, how fast it's flowing, and whether you've managed to stop it at the stopcock. A good plumber will tell you what to do while they travel — usually, run the cold taps to drain what's left in the pipes, and switch off the electricity if water is anywhere near sockets or the fuse box.

## Third: tell your neighbours and your landlord

If you live in a flat, water rarely stays in one home. Knock on the flat below and let them know — they'd rather hear it from you than discover it from their ceiling. If you rent, call your landlord or letting agent as soon as the plumber is on the way. If you own the flat, check whether the leak is in your pipework or the building's shared pipework — that decides who pays, and your building's management company needs to know either way.

## Fourth: call your insurer — after the water stops

Your insurer needs to know, but they can't stop the water, so they're fourth in the queue, not first. When you call, have the facts ready: when it happened, what burst, what you've done, and what the plumber found. Photograph everything before anything is moved or dried — damp walls, damaged floors, ruined belongings. Insurers work from evidence, and the evidence disappears as soon as the drying starts.

## The one thing to do before winter

Find your stopcock today. Then check that everyone in the household knows where it is. A burst pipe at 2am is bad enough; a burst pipe at 2am with nobody knowing how to stop the water is far worse.
