# How to Find a Trustworthy Emergency Plumber in Brighton

Nobody shops for an emergency plumber calmly. The search usually starts with water on the floor and a phone in a wet hand — and that is exactly when a bad choice is easiest to make. The trick is to do the choosing before the emergency, and to know what a trustworthy plumber looks like when you are in a hurry.

## Start with the number you save, not the number you find

Save a local plumber's number in your phone now, before anything bursts. A plumber with a Brighton number, a real local address and reviews from Brighton customers is far more likely to arrive quickly than a national call centre that sends whoever is free. Check the reviews yourself: look for recent ones, in your area, that mention emergency call-outs — not just a page of five-star praise with no detail.

## Ask the price question before they start

Before anyone picks up a tool, ask three questions: what is the call-out charge, what is the hourly rate, and is there an emergency rate for nights and weekends? A trustworthy plumber answers plainly. Be wary of anyone who will not give a figure until the work is done, or who starts work and only then mentions a higher emergency rate. Get the estimate in writing — even a short message counts — before the repair begins.

## Check the right credentials for the job

For water pipes, look for experience and insurance. For anything connected to a boiler or gas, the engineer must be Gas Safe registered — it is the law in the UK, and you can check the registration yourself. A plumber who cannot show proof of insurance is a plumber whose mistakes become your bill.

## Do not pay everything up front

A deposit for parts is normal. Full payment before the work is finished is not. Pay by card or bank transfer where you can, keep the receipt, and keep photos of the damage and the repair. If something fails a week later, that paper trail is what gets it fixed free.

## The best time to choose is today

Find your stopcock, check that it turns, and save the number of a plumber you have already checked. An emergency is stressful enough without adding a stranger to it. The five minutes you spend today is what turns a burst pipe from a disaster into a bad afternoon.
