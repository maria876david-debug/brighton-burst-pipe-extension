# Frozen Pipes in Brighton: The Rooms Most People Forget to Protect

When a cold snap hits Brighton, the pipes that burst are rarely the ones you can see. The ones that fail are the ones in the rooms people forget: the loft, the garage, the utility room, the pipe running through the cupboard under the stairs, and the outside tap. A pipe in a cold loft freezes first because nobody heats that space, and it bursts at night when nobody is watching. The protection is simple and cheap: lagging on exposed pipes, especially in unheated rooms, and the outside tap turned off and drained before winter. While you are at it, find your stopcock and check it turns - a frozen pipe you can isolate in minutes is a mop-up; one you cannot stop is a restoration job. Five minutes of checking now saves a flooded house later.
