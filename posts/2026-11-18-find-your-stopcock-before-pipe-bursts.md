# Find Your Stopcock Before a Pipe Bursts: Brighton's 5-Minute Job

The cheapest burst-pipe repair in Brighton is the one that never floods the house - and it starts with a five-minute job you can do today: find your stopcock. In most Brighton homes it is under the kitchen sink, in the hallway cupboard, or just inside the front door. Turn it clockwise and check that the water actually stops; a stopcock that has not moved in ten years can seize when you need it most. While you are there, check the pipes in the loft and under the stairs for lagging - the cold snap that bursts a pipe usually arrives with no warning. Knowing where the stopcock is, and that it works, is the difference between a mop-up and a restoration job.
