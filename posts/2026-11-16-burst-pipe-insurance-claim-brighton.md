# Burst Pipe Insurance Claim in Brighton: What to Document From Minute One

If a pipe bursts, your insurance claim starts the moment you discover the water. Before anything is moved or dried, take photos and a short video of every affected room - walls, floors, furniture, belongings. Note the time you found the leak and when the water was turned off.

Keep damaged items until the insurer has seen them, and keep every receipt for emergency repairs and drying. Call your insurer early and ask what they need from you; most policies expect you to limit further damage, so turning off the water and moving valuables is part of the claim, not a problem for it. Good documentation is the difference between a smooth claim and a long argument.
