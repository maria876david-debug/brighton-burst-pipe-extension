# Brighton Winter Checklist: Five Jobs to Do Before the First Frost

Winter in Brighton does not need a deep freeze to burst a pipe - one cold night is enough. Five small jobs before the first frost will save you a flooded house: one, lag any pipe in an unheated space - the loft, the garage, the cupboard under the stairs. Two, turn off the outside tap and drain it. Three, find your stopcock and check it actually turns, so you can stop the water in minutes if a pipe does go. Four, set the heating to come on for a short burst each day if you are away, so the house never drops to freezing. Five, check the boiler pressure and bleed any cold radiators, because a heating system that cannot circulate properly leaves the far rooms cold - and the far rooms are where the pipes are. None of these takes more than an afternoon, and none of them costs much. A burst pipe costs thousands and a winter of repairs.
